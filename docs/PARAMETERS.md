| $M_2$ | `M_{2}` | `1.0` | `0.1` to `5.0` | Effective mass of singularity 2 |
| $R_0$ | `R_{0}` | `5.0` | `2.0` to `10.0` | Initial binary separation |
| $A$ | `A` | `2.5` | `0.5` to `10.0` | Curvature well depth amplitude |
| $S$ | `S` | `1.0` | `0.0` to `5.0` | Visualization ripple amplification factor |
| $\epsilon_0$ | `e_{0}` | `0.6` | `0.1` to `2.0` | Softening parameter (prevents singularity blowup) |
| $k$ | `k` | `1.8` | `0.5` to `5.0` | Wave spatial wavenumber ($2\pi/\lambda$) |
| $\omega$ | `\omega` | `3.0` | `0.5` to `10.0` | Ripple angular frequency |
| $v$ | `v` | `2.0` | `0.5` to `5.0` | Gravitational wave propagation velocity |
